## What the patch does

- The GitHub source reads the `priority/P0` … `priority/P4` labels of an Issue or pull request and keeps the level it
  states in `github-priority:<number>`, with the conflict flag when several labels contradict each other.
- The launch queue ranks the pending launches by that level when it serves them, then by their durable sequence, so the
  most urgent ticket starts first and the oldest one starts first among the tickets of one priority.
- A launch that waits a day longer waits one step more urgent, so the least urgent ticket of a busy queue does not
  starve behind a constant flow of more urgent ones.
- The labels of the pending tickets are refreshed within bounds: at most 30 reads per polling round, the least recently
  checked first, and never the same Issue twice within two minutes. A read that fails keeps the last confirmed level.

No worker is preempted: a launch that started keeps the place it started in, and the maximum number of workers is
unchanged.
